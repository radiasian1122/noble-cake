    How It Works

    Three days from your order to your table.

    Mousse and jelly cakes have to be built in stages and set overnight. Nothing here is made ahead or frozen.

    01

    You choose

    Order from the six, or build your own. Pick a delivery date at checkout and we hold the slot straight away.

    02

    We build it

    Chiffon sponge on day one. Mousse and jelly set overnight. Shell or mirror goes on the morning it ships — usually on camera.

    03

    It travels cold

    Packed in a rigid chilled mailer on a support base and couriered overnight. Straight into the fridge, out twenty minutes before.
