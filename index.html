    How It Works

    Three days from your order to your kitchen counter.

    The order genuinely moves through these stages in sequence — nothing is made ahead and nothing sits in a freezer.

    01

    You choose

    Pick from the collection or build your own. Pick a delivery date at checkout — we hold your slot the moment you order.

    02

    We bake

    Sponge goes in the morning of your ship date. Buttercream is whipped, coloured, and put on by hand at the turntable. We film most of it.

    03

    It travels cold

    Boxed in a rigid chilled mailer with a support base, couriered overnight, and it arrives set. Fridge on arrival, out an hour before.
